Privacy Policy — PlayMatch

Effective date: November 9, 2026

PlayMatch (“the App”) is a sports app that lets players create an account, set up a profile, find courts and fields, add friends, book time, and join pickup games. This policy explains what data the App collects and what happens to it. Contact: [support email].

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address, username, display name, position, skill level, city, and sport. Your email is used to sign you in, send a password reset, and optionally send an account verification email. Your username, display name, position, skill level, city, and sport are visible to other signed-in users so they can find and add you as a friend.
  • Skill profile. You can add your age, height, and the highest level you officially played up to (for example Recreational, High School, College, Semi-Pro, or Professional). These are shown on your profile so other players can see how good you are.
  • Location. When you use the map or look for courts near you, the App uses your device location to find nearby courts. Your location is sent to OpenStreetMap’s Overpass API to look up nearby basketball courts; it is not stored on our servers. Courts you add yourself are public places and their location is stored so other players can find them.
  • Friends, bookings, games, and chat. Friend requests, friendships, court bookings, pickup games you post or join, and group chat messages in those games are stored so the App can show them to the people involved. Only players who joined a game can see or post in its group chat. If you report another player, the report is stored for review; reports are not shown inside the App.
  • Identifiers. Your account has an internal user ID. It is not shared with advertisers or anyone else.

What we do not collect

The App has no ads, no analytics SDK, and no tracking. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Third parties

  • Firebase (Google). Accounts and app data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy terms.
  • OpenStreetMap. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap; your approximate location is sent to its Overpass API only to fetch nearby courts.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from inside the App (My Profile > Delete Account). This deletes your sign-in, your profile, and your username reservation, freeing the username for reuse. Courts you added stay on the map because they are public places. Bookings, friend requests, and game messages you created remain in the system but are no longer linked to an identifiable profile. To ask for anything else to be removed, email [support email].

Children

PlayMatch is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, do not use the App.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the new policy.
